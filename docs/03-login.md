# 3) Login Screen

The login screen signs you into Smart Dashboard.

> Smart Dashboard uses your organization’s identity provider (SSO). Accounts are typically managed by your organization.

## What you see

![Login screen](./images/01-login.png)

## How sign-in works

- Click **Sign in**.
- You may be redirected to your company’s SSO page.
- After successful login, you are redirected back to Smart Dashboard.

## Common messages

- **Session expired:** You may be asked to sign in again.
- **Authentication error:** If sign-in fails, try again or contact your administrator.

## Sign out

You can sign out from the user menu (top-right) or via the tenant selection page menu.
